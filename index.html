How to read this: the heatmap shows GDOP computed from camera-to-point viewing geometry alone — no imagery needed. Amber stars are the greedy-optimized GCP set (lowest individual GDOP, picked from a dense candidate grid). Survey mode uses attached orthomosaics and sampled DTM elevations. GDOP is calculated from a simulated flight, not measured flight logs. Lower GDOP indicates stronger geometry in this model; it does not establish survey accuracy. Heights use the GeoTIFF EGM96 datum. Missing terrain is excluded. GCP placement requires field review.
